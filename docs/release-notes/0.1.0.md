# Busybodies 0.1.0

The first release. Your AI coding agents, as a little office: every coding-agent session gets a
desk, every subagent it spawns gets a tiny colleague, and you can watch them work frantically,
slack off shamelessly, and wave at you when they need you.

## Install

On an Apple Silicon Mac (macOS 14 or later):

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrMint/busybodies/main/install.sh | sh -s -- 0.1.0

Builds aren't signed by Apple yet. If macOS won't open Busybodies the first time, open
**System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (once). The README has the
details, and the extra step for the `.dmg`.

## What's in it

- **The open-floor office.** Projects are rooms off a hallway, sessions are desks, and a busy
  project grows rows of them. There's a lobby with a nap pod, a kitchen with a coffee queue, and a
  meeting room for reviews. Click a room, then a desk, to zoom in; Escape for the whole office.
- **Mochi characters.** Cut-paper puppets with springy parts, role costumes, and poses: typing,
  seven ways of slacking off, waiting on you, a failed test, a PR opened, handing work to a new
  subagent, napping, grumbling at a long build.
- **Acting.** Agents walk in when they start, take diffs to the meeting room for review, queue
  for coffee when they finish, and leave through the door.
- **Sass.** Bubbles, signs and whiteboards that react to what your agents are doing:
  affectionate, never mean to you, nothing crude.
- **Waiting on you.** A session waiting for an answer holds up a sign, its room pulses, and the
  top bar counts them. An optional hook (`tools/hooks.ts`) makes this instant.
- **The menu bar.** A mochi face shows whether anyone's busy, all idle, or waiting on you. From
  its menu: the office, compact mode (a small always-on-top window; also ⌘⌥B), About, and Quit.
- **Updates.** The app checks GitHub Releases a little after launch and every 6 hours, and
  restarts into a new version. `BUSYBODIES_NO_UPDATE=1` turns that off.

## Privacy

Busybodies reads your coding agent's own local files (its running sessions, their transcripts,
its subagents' details) read-only. Nothing you're working on leaves your machine; the only
network request is the update check. No analytics, no accounts.

## Known limits

- Apple Silicon Macs only, and unsigned, as above.
- Live sessions only: there's no history of past days yet.
- A very large day widens the office along its hallway; there's no second floor yet.
