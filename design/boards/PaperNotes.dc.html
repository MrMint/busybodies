B · Cut Paper

A floor map made of paper cut-outs: every project is a coloured zone, every session a round table, every agent a blob with a face. Flat fills, one ink outline and springy motion make it read at a glance from across the room, like a board game mid-turn.

Characters are a cut-out rig (board B6): one bean, noodle limbs, a face, a prop. Each part is a sprite; a frame object sets its angle and the engine springs it there.

A second theme

A theme is a sheet of cut-outs and five zone colours. Ink weight, springs and the reserved yellow and red stay. For the rig, a theme swaps each part's cut-out (straw hat on the bean, mitten hands, a pumpkin for the trophy) under the same part names, so every pose carries over untouched.
