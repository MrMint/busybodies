C · Putty · acted poses

The same six poses and the same frame objects as the other two styles; only the part art differs. Here the parts are clay: two balls, sausage limbs, googly eyes, a thumbed-in mouth. Frames snap with no easing, about eight a second, like stop motion shot on twos. At startup each distinct frame is rendered once (parts drawn with soft shading and a thumbprint texture) into a sprite atlas and played as a PixiJS AnimatedSprite; the engine adds squash on landing, clay crumbs and the narrator card on top. A theme re-sculpts the parts (helmet ball, mitten hands, a wrench for the trophy) and keeps every frame.
