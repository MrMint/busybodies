Busybodies · the cast

Paper puppets, one per agent. Every part is its own cut-out (body, two-joint arms, legs, eyes, brows, cheeks, mouth, costume, whatever is in each hand), and every part springs to its next value, so nothing steps. A role is a costume and a habit; busy is frantic, idle is shameless.

Hard at it

Session lead

Headset, and a tie in its project colour. Owns the desk and does the hiring.

Builder

Hard hat and hi-vis. Hammers until it compiles. Its own bench means its own worktree.

Scout

Ranger hat, binoculars. Runs to the file cabinets and comes back with a map.

Explorer

Pith helmet and a torch. Smaller the deeper it was hired; trails its scout like a duckling.

Reviewer

Round glasses, bow tie, clipboard. Lives in the meeting room. Has notes.

Verifier

Visor, badge, magnifier. Raises the green paddle. Or does not.

Designer

Beret, scarf, one brush. Steps back, squints, moves it two pixels.
