A · Pixel Tower

A cutaway tower in chunky pixel art: every project is a floor, every session a room, every agent a tiny bot with a job. It reads like a retro management game you happen to be winning, and it is the cheapest of the three to animate.

Built as sprites on one canvas. Characters are a 16 by 19 px rig acted frame by frame (board A6): each frame is a small object of part names, baked into an atlas at startup. Motion is 2 to 12 frames a second and stops between events.

A second theme

A theme is one sprite sheet and one palette. The grid, the motion rules and the two reserved colours (amber for needs you, red for error) stay put. A village turns the floors into a street of houses, so the cutaway scrolls sideways instead of stacking. For the rig, a theme redraws each part's blocks (helmet, glove, wrench for trophy) under the same part names, so every pose carries over.
