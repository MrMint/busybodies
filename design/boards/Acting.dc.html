Busybodies · acted poses

Can paper cut-outs act? Nine poses, each played from frame objects like the one printed under it. A frame names a value per part; the engine springs every part there (shoulder, elbow, brow, cheek, the thing in its hand), so the motion stays smooth and bouncy however few frames a pose has. In PixiJS v8 one puppet is a Container of about 18 sprites from one shared atlas; faces swap textures, limbs rotate, costumes are just more sprites. A theme reskins the atlas and keeps every frame.
