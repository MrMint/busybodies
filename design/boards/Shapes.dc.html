Mochi · chosen

Soft, squat and wide. Wider than it is tall, flat on its feet, face sat low and 12% bigger, and it never quite stops wobbling. It squashes and stretches further than the other bodies, which is where the springy motion pays off: the funniest runner and the best napper of the six. Same rig as before: jointed arms and legs, the same face parts, the same costumes and the same frames.
Bean · dropped. The round-2 body. Tall, and it reads a bit grown-up.
Gumdrop · dropped. A ball with shoulders. The best actor of the six, and the plainest of the last three.
Dumpling · dropped. Gumdrop crossed with mochi. Close, but mochi has more character.
Chibi · dropped. Cutest face up close, but its short arms crowd it and costumes shrink to dots.
Pear · dropped. A natural grump. Hats and headset sit loose on the narrow top.
What mochi costs, and what was done about it. Its arms start out wide of its face, so a mug reaches the cheek rather than the mouth, and held things sit beside the head; it reads fine at this size. It has no chest, so ties and vests shrink to a badge under the chin, and roles now read from hats and props. Hats and the headset are widened to fit the head.
