C · Putty

A tabletop of clay trays shot like stop motion: every project is a tray, every session a desk with a googly-eyed lead, every agent a ball of clay pinched off it. It is the warmest and most toy-like of the three, the one you would leave on a second screen just to watch.

Characters are a clay rig (board C6): two balls, sausage limbs, googly eyes. Frames snap at about eight a second with no easing, baked into an atlas at startup.

A second theme

A theme is a set of sculpted parts and five tray colours. The stop-motion timing, the clay shading and the reserved yellow and red stay. For the rig, a theme re-sculpts each part (helmet ball, mitten hands, a wrench for the trophy) under the same part names, re-bakes the atlas, and every pose carries over.
