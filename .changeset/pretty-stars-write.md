Compact mode is a live camera: it cuts to the room where something just happened (someone waiting on you, a red test, a PR, a rush, a compaction, a buried in-tray, a feud with the copier), holds it about 20 seconds, and otherwise rests on the busiest room. A strip of room tiles under the picture shows which rooms are busy, rushed or waiting on you; click one to keep the camera there.
