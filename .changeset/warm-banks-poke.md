The office theme goes full office: the cast dresses as an over-the-top corporate crew (a boss in a loud tie and headset, builders with their tie flung over a shoulder and a pencil behind the ear, an intern with a lanyard, a fleece-vested strategist with a laser pointer, a cardiganed reviewer with glasses on a chain, an accountant in a green visor, a designer in a black turtleneck), builders staple sheafs with a giant stapler and stack reams and binders instead of hammering nails and laying bricks, and the rooms get motivational posters, cubicle walls with family photos, ficus plants, fluorescent panels, a water cooler with paper cones and a mug wall. The factory keeps its construction gear.
