The bakery's crew dress the part: a head baker in the tallest toque, a bread baker in a gingham headscarf, a barista in a flat cap, a candy-maker with a lollipop in their cap, a pâtissier in a puffed toque, a taster wearing a cupcake, and a cake decorator with a tiny tiered cake on a headband. They roll dough, crank a stand mixer, stack dripping layer cakes, pipe icing and torch crème brûlée; the stations are a deck oven of croissants, a proofing drawer, a tasting table, a display counter and a tray of pastries piled to the ceiling, and the confetti is sprinkles.
