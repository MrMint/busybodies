Themes own their staging. In the factory each row of a bay is a production line: a belt across the floor with the crew along it, the foreman up in a booth, tool calls riding the belt as parts while the crew hold a kind of work, and PR crates riding the hallway's conveyor to the dock. Each theme has its own ambient life on the life clock (the office: steam off the coffee, a ceiling fan, the copier's light; the factory: a beacon while a build runs, steam vents, weld sparks) that holds still in calm mode, and the factory's crew have their own idle habits: sitting on oil drums, cards on a pallet, kicking the vending machine.
