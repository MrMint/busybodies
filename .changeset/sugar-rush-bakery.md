The Bakery theme is now a patisserie gone completely over the top: candy-stripe wallpaper and checkerboard floors in each room's colour, scalloped awnings and fairy lights, glass cases of croissants and cakes, a deck oven glowing in every room, a café with a big steaming espresso machine and a cake cabinet stacked to the ceiling, macaron poufs, and a giant cupcake to nap in. Pastries ride a conveyor down the hallway, a finished PR's cake box rides it to the pick-up hatch, the tasting table's cake turns on its stand, sprinkles drift round the cupcake and marquee bulbs chase round "Order up!". The words follow: orders, batches, BAKING and BOXED on the order board, the day's bake at closing.
