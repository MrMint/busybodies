The bakery gets the new look: its bakers wear their sweets as accessories (a tall toque, a gingham bow with a baguette through it, a cappuccino balanced on a flat cap, a lollipop cap, a cupcake hat, a tiny tiered cake on a headband) with one small brooch each, so their faces and colours show. At the benches you can see the head baker's whole face, the tills are smaller and lighter, rooms have pale checkered floors with the project's colour on the walls, and the empty corner is a café terrace with umbrellas and lollipop planters. Pose titles and a few lines use bakery words, and idle bakers sip cappuccinos, sneak macarons, lick the cake fork and nap on flour sacks.
