A new look for the crew: bigger, glossier faces with wide-set eyes, and office outfits worn as accessories (a headset and a loud tie for the lead, a pencil behind the ear and a forgotten sticky note for builders, an eyeshade and pocket protector for the verifier, and more), with the app and menu-bar icons redrawn to match. At the desks you can always see the lead's whole face, and the crew stand in a tidy row in their own room instead of piling up. The office's tools look like office things (a stapler, a crank pencil sharpener, letter trays), and pose titles and the crew's lines use each theme's own words. Rooms have pale floors with the project's colour on the walls, a patio where a bare block used to be, a livelier yard with a path, and a furnished corner office.
