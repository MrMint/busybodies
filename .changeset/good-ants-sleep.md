"busybodies": minor
---

Delight, round two: a treat appears on the desk when a room's fifth or tenth PR of the day goes up, or a session crosses a thousand lines changed (a cake in the office, a frosted hex-nut doughnut in the factory, a croquembouche in the patisserie). Desks idle for days gather a cobweb. A very long call now dozes off at the station it went to, on hold at the phone. Plum hands land on their keyboard, sheets and tools at every size, and the closer look draws less often.
