Quitting is clean on every path: the tray's Quit, ⌘Q (the app now has a menu), the Dock's Quit and logging out all stop the watcher, save the diary and exit within half a second. Closing the office window keeps Busybodies in the menu bar and takes its Dock icon away; clicking the Dock icon or opening the app again brings the office back. The office no longer freezes when one of the agent's session files holds an unexpected value, and it keeps watching when the agent's folder is removed and comes back.
