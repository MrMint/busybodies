The voice speaks the theme's language. In the factory the crew talks hard hats, the belt, crates and quotas; in the bakery it's proofing, half-baked loaves, glaze, macarons and one more espresso shot, and the wall signs and fridge notes follow suit, so the office's jokes stay in the office. The compact window's room tiles now use the theme's names for each area, and with sound on the bakery has its own quiet room tone: a warm oven hum, now and then a hiss of steam from the espresso machine, and once in a while the little bell over the shop door.
