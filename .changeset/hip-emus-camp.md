Sound, off until you turn it on in the menu bar: a desk bell when someone waits on you, a stamp for a green test, a sad two-note for a red one, a rustle for a compaction, a chime for a PR and the copier's grind on a long build, over a quiet room tone, all made in code. The menu also gets Calm office, which stills the idle cast and the sound.
